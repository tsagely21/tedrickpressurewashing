    Frequently Asked Questions

    What's the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

    Pressure washing uses high-pressure water and suits hard surfaces like concrete driveways and sidewalks. Soft washing uses low pressure with cleaning solutions, which is gentler on siding and roofs. We pick the method based on your surface.

    Can I request a quote if I don't know my square footage?

    Yes. Choose "I'm not sure" for any service and add photos if you can. We'll review your details and follow up to confirm measurements before a final price.

    How does booking approval work?

    After you send a quote request, you can choose a preferred date and time window. That's a request only: it shows as "Pending owner approval." The owner can accept it, decline it, or propose a different time. You're confirmed only after acceptance, and a confirmed time is held for you.

    Can the final quote change after inspection?

    Yes. Online estimates depend on the measurements and details you provide. The final price is confirmed after we review your property, measurements, and surface condition.
